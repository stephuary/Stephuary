Step 01

What do you actually do?

Not what you want to do. Not the refined version. Write what you actually do right now, as plainly as possible.

Format: I help [someone] do [something]
Optional. List any other ways you have said this — separated by line breaks.

Step 02

Who is this actually for?

Not everyone. Not anyone who needs it. Name the specific type of person who would get immediate value from this.

Format: People who ___
Optional but useful. Naming the exclusion sharpens the inclusion.
What is it about them that makes this relevant? What are they dealing with?
Step 03

What changes for them?

Not a feeling. Not a vague improvement. What is specifically different after working with you?

Format: So they can ___
What is their situation before they work with you?
What is measurably or meaningfully different?

Step 06

What this is pointing toward

Your statement is a signal. It tells you what kind of work this is, who it is actually for, and what it rules out. Name it clearly.

What type of work is this closest to? Advisory, consulting, coaching, done-for-you, training, creative, operational...
What type of buyer does this most naturally lead to? Individual, small team, company, founder...
Naming the boundaries keeps the direction clean.
Optional. If something still feels off, name it here so Position can address it.

Phase 01 complete
