Stephuary Phase 03 — Structure
Structure Step 1 of 5
What are the steps.
List the sequence someone moves through to get the outcome.
Keep it to the major steps. Not every micro-action.
Separate steps with commas if more than one.
What is required.
What does someone need to bring or have before this can work.
Requirements set expectations. Name them clearly.
What is delivered.
What does someone walk away with. Be specific.
The deliverable is what makes the outcome real.
What order does it follow.
Name the logic. Why does this step come before that one.
The order is the argument for why it works.
Where does it break.
What is the most likely point of failure in this system.
Honest answer only.
Naming the break point is how you prevent it.
