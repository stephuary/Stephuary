Structure · Phase 04

Make the offer operable.

This is the architecture phase. The offer already exists. Now it needs a delivery structure, a client flow, an internal workflow, and a sequence that can repeat without depending on memory.

Output
One clear delivery structure and one repeatable sequence.
Detection
Surface manual work, custom work, missing handoffs, and sequencing problems.
Next
Automation removes unnecessary manual repetition from the structure you define here.
Step 01

Offer breakdown

Break the offer into actual components

A vague offer cannot be structured. Break it into what is actually delivered.

Name the actual offer, not the category.
What should exist for the client by the end?
Use one time frame.
Separate what is delivered into real parts, not broad claims.
Step 02

Delivery sequence

Define the order of delivery

The client should move through a sequence, not a pile of steps.

Write it in sequence, separated by commas.
Payment, booking, approval, something else.
What must exist at the end for the work to count as done?
Step 03

Client journey

Map what the client sees and receives

If the client experience is missing visible moments, the delivery will feel loose even when work is happening.

Write the visible client-facing sequence, separated by commas.
Name the main communication points.
What moment needs more clarity?
Step 04

Internal workflow

Map what happens behind the scenes

The client sees the front. Structure depends on the back.

Write the internal sequence, separated by commas.
What changes hands between tools, people, or stages?
What are you remembering instead of defining?
Step 05

Bottlenecks

Identify what is too manual or too custom

This is where friction gets named instead of excused.

What repeats with no good reason?
Where are you making unnecessary variations?
Where is the sequence dragging?
What part does not repeat reliably yet?
Step 06

Simplification

Remove unnecessary steps and complexity

Structure gets stronger when extra decisions disappear.

Name the steps, decisions, or variations that should stop.
What should become a repeatable asset?
Keep only what truly benefits from judgment.
This will build the structure, detect friction, and open the repeatable sequence.
