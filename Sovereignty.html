Stephuary Phase 05 — Sovereignty
Sovereignty Step 1 of 9
What must you own.
What parts of this work cannot belong to a platform, a tool, or someone else.
The audience, the IP, the system, the relationship — what is actually yours.
Separate with commas if more than one.
What does it depend on.
Name every external thing this system needs to function.
Platforms, tools, people, permissions, third-party access.
Dependencies are risks. Name them honestly.
What breaks first.
If something fails, what is the first thing that stops working.
This is usually the most fragile dependency.
One thing. The one you already know.
What is the asset.
What has been built that has value independent of any platform or tool.
An audience you own, a document, a system, a body of work.
If the platform disappeared tomorrow, what would you still have.
What needs protection.
What is valuable enough that losing it would be material.
Protection means contracts, backups, or redundancy. Name what needs it.
What needs backup.
What would cause a disruption if it disappeared today.
Not everything. The things with no redundancy.
These need a second location, a second format, or a second person who knows them.
What increases independence.
What would reduce your dependence on any single platform, person, or condition.
Independence is built incrementally. Name one move.
What creates durability.
What would make this system continue to hold under pressure, change, or absence.
Durability means it runs without requiring everything to stay the same.
Sovereignty statement.
Complete the sentence.
This system is mine because ______.
Write what is actually true, not aspirational.
