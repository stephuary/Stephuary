What this is

A controlled access layer. No browsing, no brokered matching, no community feed.

You post what you need or what you have. Aligned peers reach out directly. Conversations move off-platform inside 48 hours.

How it works

  1. Apply. Two-paragraph written application. Reviewed by Stephanie directly.
  2. Pass. If approved, pay $1,500 for 12 months of access.
  3. Post. Structured profile: focus area, stage, what you have, what you need.
  4. Signal. Post an ask or attach a doc when you have something in motion.

What $1,500 buys you

Access. Not curation. Not matching services. Not ongoing support.

You buy the right to post, the right to reach out to other members, and the right to be reached. You use it when you have something in motion — not on a cadence.

This is a pay-for-access model. There is no guaranteed match frequency, no monthly check-in, no back-channel brokering. If you want a brokered introduction, you want a different service.

Questions

Is this a community?
No. There is no feed, no forum, no scheduled calls, no group thread. It is an access layer, not a community.
What happens if I never post a signal?
You paid for the access. You can use it or not. There is no refund for unused access past the 7-day trial.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Auto-renewal can be turned off at any time. Manual renewal is the default.
What if my application is rejected?
You’ll receive a decision within 7 days. No fee is charged on rejection. You can reapply once per 12 months.
How is this different from a networking group or LinkedIn?
Networking groups broker introductions. LinkedIn is open and uncontrolled. This Club is private, direct, and controlled only at the gate. Approved members operate peer-to-peer.
What happens to my data if I leave?
Profile deleted on request. Past match records retained for 36 months in case of dispute. See our Privacy policy.
Is this the same as Only Sometimes Club?
No. Only Sometimes Club is a distinct and separate product.
Can I see current members before applying?
No. Profiles are private until match. That is the controlled-access model.
