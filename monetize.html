Monetize Step 1 of 5
What are they paying for.
Name the thing. Not the category — the specific thing.
Be as specific as you can in one line.
What problem does it solve.
The real problem. Not the surface one.
Start with "they" if it helps.
What is the outcome.
What does someone have after this that they didn't have before.
Name the result, not the process.
What format is it delivered in.
How does someone actually receive this.
Be concrete. Format affects price.
What makes it worth paying for.
Not why you think it's good. Why someone else would pay.
The honest answer is usually the right one.
