Build the offer

Define one thing someone can buy now. Not everything you do.

Specific person, not a broad category. Not "entrepreneurs" — "freelance designers who cannot close clients."
One outcome. Not a list.
One format. Not a mix.
Single timeframe the buyer commits to.
Single number. Not a range.

  • Why it is worth the price

    Choose the version to test

    Three ways to sell the same offer. Pick one and test it this week.

    Version A
    Direct outreach
    Send this offer to three specific people this week.

    Write one message. Say what you do, who it is for, and what it costs. Send it to three people who already know you. Not strangers.

    This is the fastest path to a first payment. No funnel required.

    Version B
    Public post
    Post the one-sentence pitch to one platform. See who responds.

    Use the one-sentence pitch from your output above. Post it where your buyer already is. No thread, no story. One clear post. One clear offer.

    You are not announcing a business. You are testing a sentence.

    Version C
    One-page waitlist
    Put the offer on a page. Drive one source of traffic to it.

    Headline is the result. Subtext is who it is for and how it works. One CTA. If no one signs up after 100 views, the offer or the audience is wrong.

    Do not build the full site first. One page. One offer. One traffic source.

    One rule

    If people are not buying, one of three things is wrong: the result is not clear, the price does not match what they are willing to pay, or the format does not fit how they want to work.

    Do not fix all three at once. Fix the first weak point and test again.
