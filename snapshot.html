Common questions

What is this?

A diagnostic plus execution system for businesses that need clearer positioning, stronger structure, and a better path to conversion.

How is this different from other audits?

Most audits point out problems. This identifies the highest-return move and shows you how to use it.

How does the queue work?

You purchase first, then submit the intake. Delivery begins from intake order, not purchase order.

Who is this best for?

For businesses that feel close to working but are not converting, scaling, or positioning as strongly as they should.

When does the 48-hour window start?

When your intake is complete and submitted, not when you pay. Calendar days; if intake arrives late in the day, delivery is still measured from that submit time. Queue position (1–2 weeks) is separate from the 48-hour written pass once you are active in the queue.

What about refunds?

The after-delivery guarantee applies to the written breakdown: if it does not name at least one change you will ship, email for a full refund after you have read it. Payment processor timing applies. Installment plans follow the same Snapshot scope—see checkout terms for charge timing.

Do I get unlimited revisions?

Snapshot includes one rewritten block you declare at intake, plus the written breakdown and the 30-minute call to sequence fixes. Further rewrites or additional assets are a new scope—not part of this SKU.
