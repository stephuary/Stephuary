FAQ

Common questions

What if I don’t have a website yet?

The pass needs something buyers can see: a live URL, public profile, landing page, or a paste of your main offer or bio. No surface to review at all? Run the free diagnostic first, or ship a one-pager so there’s a real line to test.

What if I’m pre-revenue?

It fits when you’re in market with traffic, leads, or a live offer to tighten. If you aren’t testing with real buyers yet, the diagnostic is usually the better first step; Snapshot is for tightening what’s already in motion.

Is this 1:1 or async?

The written pass and the one declared rewrite are async. The 30-minute call is live 1:1 to walk the findings and lock what to do next.

What do I send you to start?

After checkout you get the intake form: your URL(s) or paste, the slice to rewrite, buyer context, and goals. The 48-hour window for the written pass starts when that intake is submitted complete, not when you pay. Queue timing (often 1–2 weeks) is separate from that 48-hour window once you’re active in the queue—see how the queue works.
