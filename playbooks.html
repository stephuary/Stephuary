If this is not working

Signal

Low replies

Fix

Targeting is wrong. You are reaching people who do not need this. Change who you are reaching, not what you are saying.

Signal

High replies, low closes

Fix

The offer is not clear or the price is not justified. Rewrite the outcome you deliver in one sentence. Make it specific enough to say yes or no to immediately.

Signal

Low volume

Fix

You are not doing enough. Not a targeting problem. Not an offer problem. Increase output. Double the daily action count and run for 7 days before reassessing.

Signal

Closes but no repeat

Fix

Delivery is not matching expectation. Tighten the scope of what you promise. A smaller promise delivered fully beats a large one delivered partially.

Signal

Inconsistent results

Fix

You are not executing the same way each time. Return to the path card. Follow the daily actions exactly. Do not modify the system while running it.
