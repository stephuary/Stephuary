System structure

The system is divided into five sections:

  1. Where your time and money are going
  2. What to change this week
  3. What you can offer and charge for
  4. Fixing execution and delivery
  5. Using AI without lowering quality

Each section:

  • focuses on one outcome
  • gives you something to complete
  • leads to a usable result
How sections stay separate

Each phase does one job. A later step assumes the earlier one is finished — it does not re-ask or re-solve the same problem.

  • Diagnostic — bottleneck, cost in plain terms, what you can use, one recommended section. No leak totals, weekly plan, offer, pricing, outreach, or AI workflows.
  • Room 1 — quantify leaks and cut them. No offers, monetization, or AI.
  • Room 2 — reallocate this week. No skill discovery, offer build, pricing, or platform strategy.
  • Room 3 — offer, buyer, price, first message. No time tracking or AI education.
  • Room 4 — execution and delivery. No new offer from scratch, no leak math, no AI stack design.
  • Room 5 — AI and distribution. No leak worksheets, weekly scheduling, or drafting a first offer from zero.
