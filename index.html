The Five Phases

  • Most ideas stay stuck because they're never fully stated. You know what you mean but you haven't written it down in a way that holds. This phase forces the idea into a structure you can actually work with.

    You'll answer five prompts. Each one pulls a different layer out of the idea. The output combines them into something clear enough to build from.

    What you do
    • Name the idea and who it's for
    • State the problem it solves in one sentence
    • Identify the outcome someone gets from it
    • Define the format it lives in
    • Confirm the next action to move it forward
    Output
    A defined idea with a clear direction and a specific next step. Ready to take into Phase 02.
    Phase 02 takes this output and builds a sellable offer from it.
    Start Capture

  • Most ideas don't translate into offers.

    You'll name what you're selling, define who it's for, state what it costs, and write a single line that makes the value clear to someone who's never heard of you.

    What you do
    • Define who is paying and what they're paying for
    • Name the specific problem this solves
    • State the outcome clearly enough to put a price on
    • Choose a format and price point
    • Write your offer in one sentence
    Output
    A defined, priced offer you can send to someone today. No pitch deck. No funnel. Just the offer, clear.
    Phase 03 takes this offer and maps it into a repeatable structure.
    Unlock Access
    Access to this phase is included in Archive.

  • Without structure, it doesn't hold.

    This phase maps your offer into a repeatable sequence. What happens first, what's required, what's delivered, and where it breaks.

    What you do
    • List the steps someone moves through to get the outcome
    • Identify what's required at each stage
    • Define what gets delivered and when
    • Name the order the sequence follows
    • Locate where the process typically breaks down
    Output
    A structured system map for your offer. Something you can hand off, replicate, or scale without starting over.
    Phase 04 takes this map and removes what doesn't need to run through you.
    Unlock Access
    Access to this phase is included in Archive.

  • If it depends on you, it doesn't scale.

    This phase finds what repeats, what slows things down, and what can come off your plate without hiring anyone or adding a new tool.

    What you do
    • Name what happens more than once in your delivery
    • Identify the friction point that costs the most time
    • Find what requires your decision but doesn't need to
    • Choose one thing to remove from your plate first
    • Map your automation in order of impact
    Output
    A ranked automation plan built from your actual workflow. Prioritized by impact, not by what's easiest to set up.
    Phase 05 takes what you've built and makes it stable, independent, and yours.
    Unlock Access
    Access to this phase is included in Archive.

  • What runs on someone else's terms isn't yours yet.

    You'll define what you've secured, what still depends on others, and the statement that makes the system actually yours.

    What you do
    • List what you've built and now own outright
    • Identify what still depends on external conditions
    • Define what stability looks like for this system
    • Name what you're building toward next
    • Write your sovereignty statement
    Output
    A sovereignty statement and a complete system map. Fewer dependencies, better leverage, a system you control.
