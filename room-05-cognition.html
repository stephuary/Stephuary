Bonus — Authority Operating System

Core structure

Signal → Authority → Income

Signal: capture what you know. Authority: publish and prove it. Income: convert attention into paid work, products, and systems.

1 — Tool system

One tool per step

  • ChatGPT — structure outlines, first drafts, fast iteration
  • Claude — edit, tighten tone, longer reasoning
  • Perplexity — answers with citations
  • Gemini — research with Google sources
  • NotebookLM — summarize and connect your own files
  • Gamma — slide-style decks from text
  • Canva — graphics for posts and thumbnails
  • Agents / automation — only after the workflow is repeatable

Finish the step in one tool. Switch tools only when the next step needs a different capability.

2 — Daily vs batch

Two ways to ship

Daily

One idea → one piece of writing.

Batch (recommended)

One 60–90 minute block: record 5–10 short voice memos, then process each into a draft.

3 — Thinking engine

Topics that force a point of view

Prompts

  • What is overhyped that I disagree with
  • What works, but is rarely discussed
  • What people repeat but misunderstand
  • What I changed my mind about
  • What I would fix first in my field
  • What wastes time with no measurable return

Contrarian angles

  • Combine two unrelated ideas
  • Apply your field to a current event
  • Challenge a dominant belief with evidence
  • Simplify something complex in one page

Generate 20 topics. Pick one.

5 — Transcription and structure

6 — Article structure

Standard order

  1. Hook
  2. Tension
  3. Breakdown
  4. Correction
  5. Implication

Each section must add a new claim or a new piece of evidence.

7 — Research and fact-check

When you cite facts

If you use numbers, trends, or external claims: use Perplexity or Gemini, confirm with at least two independent sources, and delete anything you cannot verify.

8 — Anti-slop

What “slop” is

Slop is generic text that could apply to any industry: empty intensifiers, fake balance, padded lists, and no decision.

Banned phrases (examples)

  • “in today’s world”
  • “it is important to note”
  • “various factors”
  • “this highlights”
  • “overall” / “in conclusion”

Editing prompt

You are a senior editor.

Remove AI slop: vague phrasing, filler, hedging, and sentences that could apply to any topic.

Keep the author’s voice. If a sentence is generic, rewrite it with one concrete detail or cut it.

9 — Human review

Before you publish

  • Read the whole piece without tools.
  • Delete or rewrite every sentence you would not defend in public.
  • Check that each claim has a source or is clearly labeled as opinion.

10 — Publish and distribution

Order of operations

Publish long-form first (Substack and/or YouTube). Then cut: 3–5 short written posts, 2–3 short clips. Short clips point back to the long piece.
