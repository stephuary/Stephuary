The Signal Engine

You are not creating content. You are generating signal that leads to paid work. Follow this exactly.

Step 1 — What to talk about

Choose ONE:

  • something you’ve done
  • something people ask you
  • something you understand better than most

Fill in:

“I help [person] solve [problem] so they can [result].”

Step 2 — Record

Open voice memos. Speak for 2–5 minutes:

“Here’s the problem with [topic].
Here’s why it happens.
Here’s what to do instead.”

No edits.

Step 3 — Structure

Paste into AI. Use this prompt:

Turn this into:

• Substack article (600–900 words)
• YouTube script (3–5 min)
• 3 short posts

Generate 5 titles and 2 subtitles.

Titles must follow:

• Why [X] is wrong
• The real reason [problem happens]
• Stop doing this if you want [result]

Keep my tone.
Do not add ideas.

Step 4 — Publish

Publish same day:

  • Substack
  • YouTube
  • Instagram / TikTok

Step 5 — Angles

Use these:

  • Mistake
  • Breakdown
  • Reality
  • Fix
  • Proof

Step 6 — Frequency

3–7x per week.

Do not change direction.

Step 7 — Client message
