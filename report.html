CHAPTER 04

Monetization Architecture

Price, structure, and what this leads to.

Most people underprice because their content is broad.

Charge $5–10 if:

  • broad content
  • general audience

Charge $15–30+ if:

  • defined niche
  • clear outcomes

Charge $50+ if:

  • content saves time
  • content makes money
  • content replaces trial and error

People don't pay for more content. They pay for clarity, speed, and advantage.

  • 80–90% free content
  • 10–20% high-value content
  • push annual pricing

Day 30–60:

  • refine topics
  • strengthen positioning

Day 60–90:

  • inbound starts
  • paid writing opportunities
  • consulting conversations

Most people stop too early.

As content sharpens:

  • audience becomes clearer
  • problems become specific
  • opportunities increase
