How it works.

01
Member Setup
You complete a structured profile. What you do. What you have access to. Industries you operate in. Locations you can move in. What you are building. What you are looking for.
02
Availability Controls
You set how you want to show up. Open to opportunities. Open to collaboration. Open to being contacted. Not available. You control your visibility and availability.
03
Requests
At any time, you can submit a need, an opportunity, a collaboration, a role, a placement, a deal, or a specific ask. It is placed inside the network. Not posted publicly.
04
Matching
The system evaluates alignment across members. You are shown who fits, what they do, what they have access to, and why they match. Other members may also be shown your request if it fits them.
